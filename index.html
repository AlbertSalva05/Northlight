About Northlight

Portland’s camera specialists, online since 2004

Buying a camera online should feel as confident as holding one in the shop. At Northlight Camera Co., every body, lens and light we sell is chosen and tested by working photographers, so you get honest advice rather than a sales script. Whether you are upgrading from a smartphone, building a first mirrorless kit or adding a fast prime for portraits, our specialists compare sensor size, autofocus, stabilization and battery life in plain language.

We stock full-frame and APS-C mirrorless cameras, DSLRs, medium format systems, cinema cameras and instant film, alongside lenses for every major mount. Each purchase includes a two-year warranty, free insured shipping on orders over $99 and 30-day hassle-free returns.

Our trade-in program gives fair, transparent value for your old gear, and every used item is graded, cleaned and backed by a one-year guarantee. Prefer to talk it all through? Book a free video consultation or visit our Portland studio to try equipment before you buy. Great photographs always start with the right tools — and the right people behind every single shot.
